Purchase Order has 3 common format variants.
Header (Y<200): Title + PO info (po_number, po_date, delivery_date_str, project_number).
Parties (Y≈250-350): Buyer(left) / Vendor(right).
Items Table (Y≈400-700).
Totals (Y≈750-850): subtotal, vat_amount, grand_total.
Signature (Y>850): approver_name, approver_title.

Differentiate from invoice: Buyer & Vendor are both present; has Delivery Date.
